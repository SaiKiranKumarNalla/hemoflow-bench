## Glossary

- **CFL** — cell-free layer: plasma layer near the wall depleted of RBCs.
- **Tubular pinch** — RBCs concentrate in a ring at ~0.6R.
- **Euler–Euler / mixture model** — blood as two interpenetrating continua (plasma, RBCs) or a mixture with a transported Hct.
- **LBM** — lattice Boltzmann method; D3Q19 = 3D lattice with 19 velocities; MRT = multiple-relaxation-time collision.
- **FNO / DeepONet** — neural operators that learn maps between functions (fields).
- **Multi-fidelity** — combine many cheap (L1) and few expensive (L2) simulations.
- **Encapsulation index I** — Piergiovanni 2020 normalised loading measure.
- **HI** — hemolysis index (power-law damage model).
- **EI** — elongation index (RBC deformability from ektacytometry).
