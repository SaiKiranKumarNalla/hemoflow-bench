Note: Zhao 2008 (Ann Biomed Eng 36:1130-41) is a 3D rectangular channel (100:200 um, depth not
reported) at Hct 20-30% with RBC depletion in the corner; Wu et al. 2015 (IJES 95:49-59, OpenFOAM
two-fluid, Hct 0.20, U_in 0.167/0.833 m/s) matched the recirculation zone only qualitatively.
A 2D single-phase Newtonian/Carreau model cannot represent the Hct or depth effects — only the
recirculation/reattachment length is comparable. Re=12.2 itself belongs to Wu 2015's Karino &
Goldsmith tubular case (77.5 um tube); see knowledge/12-constants-verification.md flag 1.
